# Aarav Signboard — Signage & Print Studio

A marketing website for **Aarav Signboard**, a Chennai-based signage, digital printing, and fabrication workshop. The site showcases their product portfolio — LED boards, ACP panels, brass name plates, roll-up stands, hoardings, bus wraps, garden umbrella branding, and more — along with an about page and a contact form that submits enquiries via email.


## Pages

- **Home** — Hero with image shuffle, featured work, services overview, testimonial
- **Work** — Gallery of 19 product categories with detail pages (pricing, scope, metrics, carousel)
- **Studio** — About, principles, full product listing
- **Contact** — Enquiry form with product selection, budget picker, and email submission
